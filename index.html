A letter for Dammy

My Baby Girl,

I honestly don't know where to start, because somehow you have become such a huge part of my life that it is difficult to remember what everything felt like before you.

You have changed me in ways I never expected. You make me want to be better, to grow, to love harder, to be more patient, and to become the kind of man you deserve. You bring out a side of me that I genuinely love.

And I need you to know that I appreciate you. I appreciate the little things, the big things, the way you care, the way you understand me, the way you make ordinary moments feel special. I really, really love you.

At this point, I honestly don't know what my life would look like without you in it. And I don't want to find out. You have become part of my happiness, my peace, my plans and the future I imagine for myself.

I promise you, with all my heart, that I will do my best not to break your heart. I want to love you properly. I want to make you feel safe, valued, appreciated and deeply loved. I want to make you the happiest woman I possibly can.

I want to spend time with you, make memories with you, travel with you, laugh until our stomachs hurt, have random late-night conversations, discover new places, and go on all the crazy little adventures life has waiting for us.

Most of all, I want to keep showing you — not just telling you — how much you mean to me.

I love you.
— Your Baby Boy ♡
